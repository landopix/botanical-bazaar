Shipping & Local Nursery Pickup

The Botanical Bazaar is proudly based in St. Petersburg, Florida. We specialize in rare tropical foliage, collector aroids, orchids, and medicinal plants. To ensure every customer receives pristine living specimens, we offer flexible fulfillment options including Standard Shipping and Local Nursery Pickup ($0.00 / Free).

Standard Shipping

  • Dispatch Location: Shipped with expert care directly from our nursery in St. Petersburg, FL.
  • Protective Packaging: Every plant is individually wrapped, secured with soil moisture barriers, and boxed with custom cushioning to withstand transit.
  • Weather Protection & Holds: For cold-weather destinations, complimentary heat packs and thermal insulation wraps are added. If extreme freezing or heat conditions threaten transit routes, we hold shipments until conditions safely clear.
  • Transit Rates & Carriers: Rates are calculated dynamically at checkout based on box size, weight, and destination zip code via USPS Express or UPS Ground.

Local Nursery Pickup ($0.00 / Free)

  • Location & Scheduling: Pickups take place at our St. Petersburg nursery location. After placing your order, you'll receive a confirmation with a link to choose a pickup time slot.
  • Health Guarantee & Inspection: All plants are guaranteed in top health upon pickup. Our nursery guides are happy to review care tips, potting advice, and lighting requirements before you leave.
  • Hold Policy: Orders selected for Local Nursery Pickup are reserved for up to 5 business days following order confirmation.
