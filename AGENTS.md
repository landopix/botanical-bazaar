## Plant facts and listings

Before researching plants, generating listing copy, editing product facts or adding care defaults, read `docs/PLANT-EVIDENCE-POLICY.md`. Retrieve the approved canonical Sanity plant reference and approved product link. Run the plant-listing gate. Missing evidence triggers research or a review flag; do not invent facts or extrapolate silently across taxa, preparations or animals. Preserve Shopify commercial/inventory data. Never publish an active listing edit or deploy without explicit owner authorization. Unpublished pilot fixtures are not production evidence approval.
