Shipping & Pickup

The Botanical Bazaar is proudly based in St. Petersburg, Florida. Because we grow and sell living plants, we currently offer local pickup only to ensure each plant arrives in excellent condition. We're exploring shipping options and will update this page once we can confidently mail live plants.

Local Pickup

  • Scheduling: After placing your order online, you'll receive a confirmation email with a link to schedule an appointment. We offer flexible pickup windows throughout the week.
  • Location: Pickups are at our nursery in south St. Petersburg or at select Tampa Bay markets. Exact location details are provided when you schedule.
  • Care Tips: At pickup we'll review care instructions and answer any questions. Bring your confirmation email and a suitable vehicle to transport your plants safely.
  • Hold Policy: We'll hold plants for up to 48 hours after confirmation. If you need more time, let us know and we'll do our best to accommodate.

Shipping (Coming Soon)

We're committed to finding a sustainable and reliable way to ship live plants without compromising their health. Our team is testing insulated packaging, heat packs and express delivery routes. Once we're satisfied that plants can travel safely, we'll launch shipping and share full details here, including rates and regions served. In the meantime, we appreciate your support of our local pickup program.
